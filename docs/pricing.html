FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

MVPs: 4-6 weeks. Business platforms: 8-12 weeks. Enterprise systems: 3-6 months depending on scope. We provide a detailed timeline during our discovery phase.

Do you work with government entities?

Yes. We understand public sector procurement, PFMA/MFMA compliance, and CIDB requirements. Several of our founders have direct government experience.

What technologies do you use?

React, Next.js, Python, FastAPI, Node.js, Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. We choose the right stack for your needs.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Absolutely. We integrate with legacy systems, ERPs, CRMs, and databases. Our APIs are designed for interoperability.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. For projects over R100,000 we offer milestone-based payments (e.g., 40% upfront, 30% mid-project, 30% on delivery). Contact us to discuss terms.

What about POPIA compliance?

POPIA compliance is built into every project. We conduct data protection impact assessments and ensure all systems meet South African privacy requirements from day one.
