About Us

More Than a Software Company

Founded by Dakalo Mashau in collaboration with co-founder Koketso Raphasha, Kirov Dynamics Technology is a technology, AI, cybersecurity, and smart infrastructure company designed to deliver digital solutions for businesses, governments, and engineering organisations — built for Africa, ready for the world.

Our rare combination of software engineering, AI expertise, government policy understanding, and civil engineering experience positions us to serve across sectors that most technology companies simply cannot reach.
