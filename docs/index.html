About Us

More Than a Software Company

Founded by Koketso Raphasha, Kirov Dynamics Technology is a technology, AI, cybersecurity, and smart infrastructure company designed to deliver digital solutions for businesses, governments, and engineering organisations — built for Africa, ready for the world.

Our rare combination of software engineering, AI expertise, government policy understanding, and civil engineering experience positions us to serve across sectors that most technology companies simply cannot reach.

Software & AI

Full-stack development, AI systems, cybersecurity, and cloud infrastructure.

Government & Policy

Public sector relationships, procurement strategy, and GovTech delivery.

Civil Engineering

Construction management, infrastructure, and smart engineering solutions.

Our Structure

Nine Specialised Divisions

Each division is purpose-built to serve a critical domain — giving our clients integrated, expert solutions across every layer of the digital economy.

Division 01

Digital Transformation

Helping South African businesses modernise legacy systems, streamline operations, and transition to digital-first strategies through technology roadmaps and change management.

Division 02

AI Solutions

Building intelligent systems that automate processes, generate insights, and create competitive advantages — from AI chatbots to custom machine learning pipelines for African markets.

Division 03

Cybersecurity

Protecting South African digital infrastructure with vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, SOC dashboards, and POPIA-aligned compliance programmes.

Division 04

Smart Infrastructure

Integrating digital technology with physical infrastructure — BIM, digital twins, IoT monitoring, drone inspections, and smart building systems for South African cities.

Division 05

Engineering Technology

Software built for South African engineers and construction professionals — site management apps, structural monitoring, Mining 4.0 systems, and construction ERP platforms.

Division 06

Government Technology

Citizen portals, permit systems, emergency response platforms, smart city dashboards, and digital workflows for South African municipalities and government departments.

Division 07

Consulting

Expert advisory for digital transformation, AI readiness, POPIA & cybersecurity maturity, cloud modernisation, and technology architecture reviews for African organisations.

Division 08

Data & Intelligence

Data engineering, business intelligence dashboards, predictive analytics, and AI-powered reporting that turns raw data into strategic decisions for African enterprises.

Division 09

Cloud & Platform Engineering

Cloud migration, CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes orchestration, and infrastructure automation — building scalable, resilient platforms on AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.
