Before & After

What Changes When Kirov Gets Involved

Real transformation doesn't need technical language. Here's what your team actually experiences.

✕ Before Kirov

5 employees manually capture customer information into spreadsheets every day.

Reports take 8 hours to compile. By the time they're ready, the data is stale.

Customer enquiries arrive via WhatsApp, email and phone — nobody knows who responded to what.

Vehicles tracked by calling drivers. No real-time visibility, no fuel records, no history.

No backups. One failed hard drive means permanent data loss.

Staff time consumed by tasks that add no strategic value.

✓ After Kirov

Customer submits one form. Data enters the system automatically. Staff get notified instantly.

Reports generate automatically overnight. Dashboard is live by 7am, every morning.

All enquiries route into one system. Every conversation is tracked, assigned and followed up.

Live fleet dashboard shows every vehicle, fuel level, route and driver report in real time.

Automated daily backups. Disaster recovery tested monthly. Zero data loss risk.

Staff focus on decisions and customers — not on capturing, chasing or compiling.
