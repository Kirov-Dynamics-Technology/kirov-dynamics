Product names, logos and screenshots remain the property of their respective owners. If you own a mark that appears here without authorisation, tell us and we will deal with it under section 5.

3. Content you provide to us

You keep all rights in the material you send us, including your data, documents, processes, images and know-how. By sending it to us you grant us a licence to use it only for the purpose it was sent for: to assess, design, build, host, secure, back up and support the work, and to keep the engagement records the law expects us to keep.

That licence is not a transfer of ownership, and it ends when we no longer need the material, except to the extent it is embedded in something we are obliged to retain. Please check you are entitled to send us what you send, and do not send us information you are not permitted to disclose.

4. What we build for you

Under a signed agreement, you own the bespoke deliverables created specifically for you, and the data in the system. We retain our own pre-existing tools, libraries, templates, tooling and general know-how, and anything that is not a bespoke deliverable. Where our deliverables contain reusable components, you have a perpetual licence to use them exactly as delivered, with no ongoing fee for that use.

Open-source components we use remain under their own licences, and we will tell you which ones a deliverable depends on where that matters to you.

5. Reporting suspected infringement

If you believe that material on this site infringes your copyright, trade mark, right of publicity or other right, tell us. Send an email to info@kirovdynamics.co.za with the subject line "Infringement notice" and include:

  • your full name and contact details, and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them;
  • the exact URL of the material you are objecting to;
  • a description of the work you own and where it is registered if you hold a registration;
  • why you believe the use is not authorised;
  • the information reasonably needed for us to contact you, such as a telephone number or postal address.

If part of a work has been licensed to us, please say so, with permission to contact the licensor, and we will pass the matter to them.

6. What we do when we receive a notice

We review every notice. Where a notice is complete and appears to be well founded, we will remove or disable the material and tell the person who sent it. Where it is not clear, incomplete, or looks like it is aimed at content we do not control, we will say so and explain why.

If you believe we removed material by mistake, you may send a counter-notice to the same address with the same information, and we will forward it to the party who complained. We may restore the material, in whole or in part, if the complaint is withdrawn or is not substantiated.

7. Repeat infringers

We apply the same policy consistently. In clear cases of infringement we remove the material, and in repeated cases we will restrict access to the person responsible. We keep records of formal notices.

8. Misrepresentation

Do not send a notice on behalf of someone else without their authority, and do not claim ownership of work you do not own. We will pass on the details of any person found to have done so, and may take action against false or abusive claims.

9. Feedback

If you send us feedback, a suggestion or an idea without asking us to keep it secret, you give us permission to use it freely and without restriction or payment, provided we do not identify you or present it as confidential. Nothing here transfers ownership of your data or your business.

10. Questions

Any question about intellectual property on this site can go to info@kirovdynamics.co.za. How we handle your personal information is set out in the Privacy Policy, and the terms that govern your use of this site are in the Terms of Use.

A note on enforceability. We can remove content that infringes our own rights, and we follow a consistent process for third-party complaints. We cannot adjudicate a dispute between you and someone else, and we do not make findings about ownership on evidence a short email cannot settle.
