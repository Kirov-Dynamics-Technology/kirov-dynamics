Construction ERP

CIDB-compliant ERP system that streamlines tender management, project tracking, procurement, and B-BBEE reporting for construction companies.

The Challenge

A CIDB-registered construction company was managing tenders, projects, procurement, and compliance through spreadsheets and paper-based systems. Tender preparation required manually pulling data from multiple sources, taking 2-3 weeks per submission. Project managers had no real-time view of budgets, schedules, or material costs.

B-BBEE scorecard tracking was entirely manual, and the finance team spent days each month reconciling project costs against invoicing. The company was losing bids because they couldn't respond quickly enough to tender opportunities.

Our Solution

We built a unified ERP platform specifically designed for CIDB-registered construction companies in South Africa.

  • Tender Management: Automated tender discovery, document assembly, and submission tracking with CIDB grading validation
  • Project Dashboard: Real-time view of project timelines, budgets, milestones, and resource allocation
  • Procurement Module: Supplier management, PO generation, and material cost tracking integrated with project budgets
  • B-BBEE Engine: Automatic scorecard calculation with spend tracking across ownership, management, skills development, and enterprise development
  • Document Vault: Centralised storage for tender documents, COCs, insurance certificates, and compliance records
  • Financial Integration: Syncs with Xero and Sage for invoicing and cost reconciliation

Business Impact

The ERP transformed the company's ability to compete for contracts. Tender preparation dropped from 2-3 weeks to 3-4 days, allowing them to bid on 3x more tenders each quarter. The real-time project dashboard means management can identify budget overruns before they become critical.

Procurement automation has reduced material cost overruns by 25%, and the B-BBEE module ensures they maintain their Level 2 contributor status without dedicated compliance staff. The platform has paid for itself many times over in saved hours and won contracts.
