If you read nothing else on this page, the above is the whole of our automated data collection. The rest of this document explains the few places where information can still arise, which is unavoidable for any website.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Kirov Dynamics Technology (Pty) Ltd ("Kirov Dynamics", "we", "us") is the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy. We are a company registered in South Africa and we handle your information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Our contact details appear at the foot of every page on this site. Queries about this policy, or a request to exercise any of your rights, can be sent to info@kirovdynamics.co.za and will be answered in writing.

2. Information this website collects on its own

We have deliberately built this site so that visiting it does not create a record of you. Specifically:

  • No cookies. The site sets no cookies, first-party or otherwise.
  • Cookieless analytics. We use Umami, a self-hosted, privacy-friendly analytics tool, to count visits and page views in aggregate. Umami runs without cookies and without visitor identifiers: it does not profile you, does not build a personal profile, and cannot identify you. The only technical data involved is a short-lived count of page views, which keeps your visits anonymous.
  • One contact form. The homepage has a single contact form ("Book Free Problem Check"). When you submit it, the details you enter (first name, last name, email, company, and your message) are transmitted over HTTPS to Formspree — a separate, third-party form provider — which forwards them to us by email. Neither Formspree nor we use that information for marketing lists or any other purpose.
  • No embedded content. We do not embed videos, maps, social widgets or third-party iframes, because each of those would report your visit to its own operator. The analytics script described above is the only third-party request the site can make.

The one piece of data our site stores, and why

Every page sets a single value in your browser's session storage, under the key rth:last. It holds the path of the page you were last on so that a page refresh returns you to the homepage instead of trapping you on a sub-page. It is:

  • used only for that one function, with no other purpose;
  • never transmitted to us or to anyone else;
  • automatically deleted when you close the tab or window, because session storage does not survive the session.

It cannot identify you, it is not shared, and blocking storage removes it with no effect on the site. You can clear it at any time through your browser's site-data controls.

3. Information our hosting provider handles

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub processes technical connection information in order to serve you the page, which can include your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and your browser's user-agent string. This is done on GitHub's infrastructure, which is outside South Africa, so it involves a cross-border transfer of that limited technical data. We do not add to it, and we do not ask GitHub to keep anything beyond its standard operational logs.

  • Analytics (Umami). Our analytics tool is hosted by us (self-hosted, cookieless). It receives a page view even when you have not typed anything in, but is configured to track nothing identifying: no cookies, no visits are tied to you as a person.
  • Contact form (Formspree). The homepage contact form submits to Formspree, a United States-based form service. Because Formspree's servers are outside South Africa, submitting the form involves a cross-border transfer of the information you type in §2. Formspree is bound to process it only to deliver it to us, and to retain it for only the short period needed for that delivery. You can equally reach us directly by email at the address below and avoid the form entirely.

4. Images supplied by Unsplash

The photographs on our homepage are served from Unsplash's image delivery network rather than from our own server. When your browser fetches one of those images, Unsplash receives the request, which necessarily includes your IP address and the page you loaded it from. We have no access to that information and no control over Unsplash's own processing of it. Every other image on this site is hosted by us.

We have self-hosted our web fonts for the same reason: loading a font from a third-party server hands that provider your IP address. The fonts you are reading this with are served from this domain and go nowhere else.

8. Who else can see it

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes. We disclose it only where necessary and lawful, namely to:

9. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to deal with your request and any resulting engagement, and record-keeping information for the period the law requires, commonly five years for accounting records under the Tax Administration Act. Information that has no further purpose is deleted or anonymised when that purpose ends.

10. How we protect it

We apply technical and organisational measures proportionate to the risk: encryption in transit (TLS) wherever data is sent or served, access restricted to the people who need it, encryption at rest on systems holding client data, least-privilege administration, logging of administrative access, patching and vulnerability management, and confidentiality obligations in every contract we sign. No system is perfectly secure, and we will never ask you to send credentials or sensitive production data over ordinary email.
